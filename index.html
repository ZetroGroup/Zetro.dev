Welcome to the Zetro Content Delivery Network.

What is Zetro.dev?

ZDN is a high-performance CDN and hosting network designed by HARMNY GROUP. Unlike standard cdn hosting, we don't host your full application, instead we deliver assets you may need for you. We do not collect any personal info when you use our network.

Our Infrastructure:
  • Global Node distribution
  • Secure location detection for low latency results
  • Delivers your important application scripts fast.
  • Works via WSS, protecting delivery from most network firewalls.

Quick Info:
Architecture: Distributed Mesh
Protocol: WSS, TCP
Optimization: Brotli, Gzip, Image Compression
